## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-07

The first release.

### Added

- Schedule tab: ongoing and upcoming events, end content, and livestreams for Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, and Zenless Zone Zero, with live countdowns. Entries with an announcement open it in the browser; estimated times are marked.
- Codes tab: active redeem codes to copy, a link to each game's redemption page, and a redeemed mark.
- Calendar tab: daily, weekly, and periodic chore checklists that reset at the server's reset time, and a month calendar of the days they were finished.
- Settings: the games you play, the server for each game, the chores to track, the language (English or Korean), and seconds in countdowns.
- A notice when a new version is released; the app does not update itself.
- Schedules and codes are downloaded from a data file published with GitHub Pages and cached for offline use.
